Mental Maths Race – Privacy PolicyLast updated: 10 November 2026

Mental Maths Race is a maths game for children. We have designed it to collect as little information as possible. We do not ask for or collect children's names, email addresses, locations, photos or contacts, and there are no accounts to sign up for.

Information kept on your device

The app stores game progress on the device: the player names you type in (a first name or nickname is enough), scores, coins, levels, achievements and settings. This stays on the device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

iCloud backup (optional)

If iCloud backup is turned on in the app's Settings, game progress is copied to your own iCloud account through Apple so it can follow you to your other devices. We cannot see this data. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the app sends scores and achievements to Game Center for leaderboards, and uses it to find opponents for online matches. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Online matches only send in-game scores between the two players; there is no chat.

If a player opens the Friends tab of the Daily Challenge leaderboard, iOS first asks for permission to see their Game Center friends. If they allow it, the app reads the friends list from Game Center only to show those friends' scores on that leaderboard. The list is not stored by the app or sent anywhere else, and access can be turned off at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and to users under the age of consent, and limited to ads rated suitable for general audiences. This means Google does not show personalised (interest-based) ads, and does not use the advertising identifier for tracking. To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the device's IP address, device type and operating system. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads appear in three places: a small banner at the bottom of the home screen, occasionally between rounds (never during a game), and an optional "watch an ad for coins" button in the Store. A grown-up can remove all ads with a one-off "Remove ads" purchase.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see payment details. Purchases and links out of the app are protected by a grown-up check.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a daily reminder on the device. Reminders are created on the device and no data is sent to us.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and the GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@mentalmathsrace.com. See also the support page.
