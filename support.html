Mental Maths Race – Support

Mental Maths Race is a quick-fire maths game for iPhone: practise times tables, race a friend on one screen, play online through Game Center, and take on a new Daily Challenge every day.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@mentalmathsrace.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you're using. We aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I remove ads?
In the app, open the Store and choose "Remove ads". A grown-up check appears first. It's a one-off purchase.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see ads.
Open the Store and tap "Restore purchases", using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can more than one child play on the same iPhone?
Yes. Tap the player name at the top of the home screen to add or switch players. Each player has their own coins, levels and stats.

How do I move progress to a new iPhone?
Turn on "Sync with iCloud" in the app's Settings on both devices, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Online play or leaderboards aren't working.
Online matches and leaderboards need Game Center. Check you're signed in under Settings → Game Center on the iPhone. Only the player linked to the Game Center account posts scores.

How do I start again?
In the app's Settings, choose "Reset progress". This clears stats, scores and Daily Challenge history for the current player; coins and powerups are kept.
